# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Three audiences read the same audit report:
- **Site owners and clients**, often non-technical: want to know how healthy the site is, whether it's ready to launch, and what matters most.
- **Developers** who fix the issues: need each finding's evidence (URLs, `file:line`) and the exact fix.
- **SEO leads and agencies** who send the report to clients as a deliverable.

## Product Purpose
`seo-supervisor` is a Claude skill that audits, reviews and fixes a website's SEO. Its audit ends in a report generated by `scripts/render-report.mjs` from `seo-audit/seo-audit.json`. The report has to answer, at a glance: how healthy is this site (a 0–100 score plus a score per area), what blocks it, and what to do first. Then it lets each audience go deeper. Full per-finding detail is also written to `seo-audit/seo-audit.md`. After a fix run, the same report shows what changed and how each fix was verified.

## Positioning
Every number in the report is traceable: scores come from the findings and the checked rules, every finding cites evidence and a rule ID, and anything the audit couldn't check is listed instead of guessed.

## Operating Context
Generated by an AI agent at the end of an audit or fix run; opened locally in a browser from the project's `seo-audit/` folder, emailed or handed to clients, and re-opened after each fix run. Sites range from a handful of pages to tens of thousands; a report may hold zero to a few hundred findings, each with up to 100 evidence rows.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- One self-contained HTML file: no network requests, no external fonts, scripts or images; it must open offline and from email attachments.
- Generated by Node 20+ with zero dependencies, from the JSON shape in `report-format.md` (or raw crawler / content-scan output).
- Must render light and dark, work at phone width, and print sensibly.
- Data available per run: site meta, run meta, summary, findings (id, rule, severity, category, status, title, what, why, evidence, count, fix, decision, fixed), decisions, passed rules, notChecked, notes.
- Severity scale: blocker, high, needs-decision, medium, low. Statuses: open, needs-decision, in-progress, fixed, wont-fix.

## Brand Commitments
Neutral: no agency or company branding, ever. It's the skill's own report for everyone who uses it.

## Evidence on Hand
- `examples/sample-audit.json` (synthetic, labelled example data) and the rendered `examples/sample-report.html`.
- A real audit of a 338-page local-business site, kept outside the skill.
- There is no fabricated benchmark or industry average to compare scores against; the report must not imply one.

## Product Principles
- Summary before detail: the first screen answers "how healthy, what blocks it, what first".
- Every score is explainable from the findings beneath it.
- Evidence over opinion: findings show where, why and how to fix.
- Honest limits: unchecked areas are shown, never hidden or guessed.
- One report through the whole loop: audit, fix, re-check.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-operable tabs and filters, status never by colour alone, readable at 200% zoom.
