# Report a vulnerability

> **Draft** (sample data): approve the policy and add the security contact before this page goes live.

If you think you've found a security problem in one of our services, please tell us privately first. We'll work with you to understand it and fix it.

## How to report it

Email **[security contact — the owner adds this]** with:

- the service and the address where you found the problem;
- the steps to reproduce it;
- what you think an attacker could do with it.

Please don't open a public issue or post about it until we've fixed it.

## What we do with your report

- We confirm we've received it within 3 working days.
- We tell you whether we can reproduce it, and keep you updated until it's fixed.
- With your permission, we thank you by name once the fix is live.

## In scope

- example-security.example and its subdomains
- the Example Security API

## Out of scope

- reports from automated scanners without a working example;
- missing security headers that have no practical impact;
- denial of service and social engineering.

## Safe harbour

We won't take legal action against you for research done in good faith within this policy: don't access more data than you need to show the problem, don't change or delete data, and give us reasonable time to fix it before you share details.
