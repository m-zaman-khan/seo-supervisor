SEO Supervisor

Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026

SEO Supervisor is a free, open-source tool (MIT licence) maintained by Zaman Khan. It runs entirely on your own computer, inside your coding agent. It has no server, no user accounts and no telemetry.

The short version

  • Google data you connect is read-only (the one exception: submitting your sitemap, only when you ask), goes from Google straight to your computer, and stays there.
  • We never receive, store, sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • You can remove access at any time, and delete everything by deleting a folder.

Google data SEO Supervisor can access

Only if you choose "Sign in with Google" (or connect your own Google client), and only with these read-only permissions:

  • Google Search Console (webmasters.readonly): the list of your Search Console properties, search performance (clicks, impressions, click-through rate and position by page and query), URL Inspection results for key pages, and the status of your submitted sitemaps.
  • Google Analytics (analytics.readonly): the list of your Analytics properties and their web streams, and organic sessions by landing page.

SEO Supervisor doesn't ask for your name, email address or contacts.

One exception you control: if you ask SEO Supervisor to submit your sitemap (submit-sitemap --yes), it first asks Google for permission to manage your Search Console sitemaps (webmasters). It uses that permission only to submit the sitemap you named and read back its status. It never adds or removes properties, changes users or settings, or deletes sitemaps, and it never submits anything without your yes. Otherwise it can't change anything in your Google accounts.

How the data is used

Only to build the SEO report you asked for, for the website you named: showing the search traffic of each page, finding pages Google hasn't indexed, spotting sitemap errors, and ordering fixes so the pages with the most traffic come first. It isn't used for anything else.

Where the data is stored

  • Your sign-in (the access and refresh tokens Google issues) is saved in ~/.seo-supervisor/google.json in your home folder. SEO Supervisor makes the file readable only by your user account where your system allows it (on Windows, through Windows' own file permissions); when that doesn't work, the connect step says so, and the file is as private as the folder it's in. It's never written into your project.
  • The results are saved in your project's seo-audit/ folder, as the report and its data files.
  • Nothing is sent to the maintainers or to any server of ours. There isn't one.

Your coding agent

SEO Supervisor runs inside a coding agent you chose, such as Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. When that agent reads the report files on your computer to explain or fix things, the agent's own provider processes that content under its own terms and privacy policy. SEO Supervisor doesn't send your Google data anywhere else.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent, transfer or share your data with anyone. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Run node google.mjs disconnect from SEO Supervisor's scripts folder. It withdraws the access at Google and deletes the saved sign-in.
  • Or remove "SEO Supervisor" at myaccount.google.com/connections. It stops working at once.
  • Delete the seo-audit/ folder in your project to delete the results, and ~/.seo-supervisor/ to delete any saved sign-ins and keys.

Other optional services

Some features contact other services, each only after you say yes: Google PageSpeed Insights and the Chrome UX Report (your public page addresses), IndexNow (the addresses of pages that changed), Bing Webmaster Tools, Open PageRank and Ahrefs (your site's address and the domain names you choose, using API keys you provide; and, only if you choose to submit them, your own page addresses to Bing), your own Matomo or PostHog (a request for your search visits, using the token you provide), your own Microsoft Clarity project (a request for the last 3 days' visitor numbers by page, using the token you provide; nothing about your site is sent), Google's Search Status Dashboard (a plain read of its public list of ranking updates; nothing about your site), the pages that link to your site (each is visited once to see whether the link is still there), and your agent's web search (search phrases and your brand name). Their own privacy policies apply. Keys you provide are saved in ~/.seo-supervisor/ on your computer.

Update notices

At most once a week, SEO Supervisor asks npm (registry.npmjs.org) for the latest version number of the seo-supervisor package, so it can tell you when an update is out. Only the package name is sent; npm sees the request like any download. Turn it off with node update-check.mjs off.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SEO Supervisor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

SEO Supervisor is a developer tool and isn't directed at children under 13.
